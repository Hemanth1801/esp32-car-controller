Add your camera later

Prepared ESP32-CAM companions are detected automatically on this Wi-Fi. Power on the paired camera and its video will appear; choose Live car when ready. Standard Wi-Fi cameras need their JPEG/MJPEG address once, then reconnect automatically.

For an ESP32-CAM or Wi-Fi camera, enter its local JPEG/MJPEG stream address. The setup page or an RTSP address will not work. The computer fetches the video and forwards it over the same trusted HTTPS connection.

No camera configured. Check its serial output or router device list for its address.

Alternatively, on a phone mounted on the car, open the camera uplink, allow its rear camera and keep that page visible.

The camera view is a single image shown to both eyes. Head movement does not turn the camera. The Quest reads up to 10 frames per second; use a small camera resolution, such as 640 × 360 or 640 × 480. Video age measures delivery freshness, not guaranteed end-to-end camera latency.
