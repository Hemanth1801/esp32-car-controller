ESP / CAR · LOCAL PHONE SETUP

Turn your phone into the steering wheel.

The controller stays on your computer. Your phone needs trusted HTTPS to read its tilt sensors.

  1. Download this computer's phone certificate
  2. iPhone/iPad: allow the profile download. In Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, install the downloaded ESP Car Phone CA profile. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings: enable full trust for this CA.
  3. Android: in Settings → Security/Privacy → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate, install the downloaded certificate. Names vary by phone. The phone may request its screen lock.
  4. Open the phone controller
    Use Safari or Chrome, tap Enable tilt and allow motion access. Hold the phone comfortably, tap Set centre, then Arm.

Hold the right half to accelerate, left half to reverse. Release to stop. Tilt left/right to steer. Both halves together, interrupted touches, stale sensors, hiding the page, and lost connection disarm the car.

This CA is generated only for this computer. Keep its private key in .mobile/ private. Remove the installed CA from your phone when you no longer use the controller. Do not install unrelated certificates.

If the controller will not open, keep the computer's start-mobile.cmd window open. The ESP, computer and phone must share the same local Wi-Fi network, and Windows Firewall must allow the two phone ports.
